Planning Your Rhinoplasty: From First Consultation to Surgery Day

A good rhinoplasty result is decided long before the operation itself. The consultation is where your surgeon studies your nose from every angle, checks how you breathe, and talks honestly about what change would suit your face. If you are researching rhinoplasty surgery in islamabad, treat that first appointment as the real starting point of the treatment, not a formality.

What happens at the consultation

Expect photographs, an examination of the internal structures, and a discussion about your medical history. Surgeons look at skin thickness, cartilage strength and the relationship between your nose, chin and forehead. Bring reference photos if you like, but expect guidance on what is realistic for your own anatomy rather than a copy of someone else's nose.

The plan you should leave with

Before booking, you should know which approach is proposed (open or closed), whether grafts might be needed, the expected downtime, and the total cost in writing. A clear plan also covers who to contact after surgery and when your follow-up visits will be.

Preparing for surgery day

You will usually be asked to complete basic medical tests, stop certain medications, and avoid smoking in the run-up to surgery. Arrange a few quiet days afterwards: most patients keep their head elevated, rest, and let the splint do its work for the first week.
